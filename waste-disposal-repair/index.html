PRO TIPS

Disposal Care Tips

Never Put Down

  • • Fibrous food: celery, corn husks, onion skins — they wrap impellers.
  • • Grease and oil — they solidify in the line downstream.
  • • Bones, shells, and non-food waste — they damage impellers.

Best Practices

  • • Run cold water while operating — keeps grease solid for easier grinding.
  • • Run it for 15 seconds after grinding to clear the chamber.
  • • Clean the splash guard monthly — that's where smells live.
