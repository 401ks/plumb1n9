Our Story

Built by dispatchers, for homeowners.

SIX7 Plumbing started in a small garage in Mt Lavinia with one van and a dream: to make emergency plumbing fair, fast, and transparent. We've grown to a 21-area network, but our roots are still local.

We don't do the plumbing ourselves. We verify your problem, confirm the rate and ETA before we move, and send a vetted independent technician. You pay after you approve the work.
