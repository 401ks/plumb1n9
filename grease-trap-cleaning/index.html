PRO TIPS

Keep Your Grease Trap Working

Scrape Plates First

  • •Remove food solids before washing. Solids are the #1 cause of premature trap fill.
  • •No hot grease down drains — let fats cool and bin them.
  • •Prevention is cheaper than repair — always.

Monitor Water Level

  • •Check the inlet tee weekly. If water is above 75% capacity, schedule a clean.
  • •Keep a cleaning log — inspectors may ask for your maintenance history.
  • •Schedule during off-hours to avoid business disruption.
