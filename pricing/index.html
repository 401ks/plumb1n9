Dispatch & Pricing FAQ

What is the LKR 5,000 deployment fee?

It covers immediate vehicle dispatch, fuel, and the contractor's first-hour diagnostic. It guarantees your 60-minute arrival slot. The fee is confirmed via WhatsApp before any contractor is deployed.

How are hourly rates confirmed?

Rates are confirmed via WhatsApp before deployment. Standard plumbing is LKR 3,000/hr, wastewater blockages LKR 5,000/hr, and sewer/biohazard work LKR 8,000/hr with a hazard premium.

Why is sewage work so expensive?

Sewage work requires biohazard PPE, specialized equipment (jetting, cameras, bowsers), and carries health risks. The LKR 8,000/hr hazard rate covers mobilization and safety.

What if I think the price is unfair after the job?

You approve the scope before dispatch, so there are no surprises. Under our zero-tolerance dispute policy, post-service price disputes are not entertained. See full rates above
