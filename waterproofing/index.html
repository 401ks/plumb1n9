MYTH BUSTED
MYTH

"Any paint can waterproof a surface."

REALITY

Waterproofing membranes are engineered products — paint is cosmetic, not structural.

FIELD NOTE

FROM 2,716+ DISPATCHES: Balcony and bathroom waterproofing are the two most common jobs — both fail at the edges, not the center.

Cause

Skipping surface prep, using wrong membrane for the area, and poor application cause most waterproofing failures.

How We Fix It

Surface prep, correct membrane selection, proper application, flood test after curing.

PRO TIP

If your bathroom floor feels soft or spongy, the waterproofing may have failed. Get it checked before the tiles crack.

WHY NOW

Failed waterproofing damages ceilings, walls, and structural elements. Every month you wait adds to the repair cost.
