PRO TIPS

Shower Installation Tips

Before We Arrive

  • •Have your shower set ready — we'll inspect it before fitting.
  • •Clear the bathroom area so we can access the wall freely.
  • •Know where your water shut-off is — we'll need to isolate during fitting.

After Installation

  • •Run the shower for 2 minutes and check for drips at connections.
  • •Keep the bathroom ventilated — the silicone needs 24 hours to cure.
  • •Report any leaks within 48 hours — they're covered under warranty.
