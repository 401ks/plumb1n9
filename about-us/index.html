Why We Built The Dispatch Model

Homeowners were tired of unreliable handymen, hidden fees, and ghosting. Plumbers were tired of chasing payments and tire-kickers. SIX7 bridges the gap. We handle the dispatch, the vetting, and the legal terms. Our contractors handle the tools.

Every contractor in our network is licensed, insured, and background-checked before they are ever deployed to your property. That is the difference between calling "a plumber" and calling the SIX7 dispatch network.

We standardize what was previously chaotic — one pricing model (LKR 5,000 deployment fee + LKR 3,000/hr), one arrival promise (60 minutes for verified emergencies), and one guarantee (the 6-month workmanship shield).

How The Network Operates

  • You WhatsApp us a 10-second video of the issue
  • We verify the emergency and quote the exact scope
  • We deploy the nearest vetted contractor — the right specialist for the job
  • The contractor isolates the issue and completes the repair
  • You pay the transparent hourly rate only after you approve the work

No middlemen playing matchmaker with anonymous tradespeople. No guesswork on cost. A managed network that treats every property like its own.
