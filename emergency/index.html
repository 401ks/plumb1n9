Emergency FAQ

What exactly is the callout fee?
A travel + assessment fee from LKR 2,500 (0–5 km). It covers fuel, on-site diagnostics and pressure testing. It is quoted before dispatch and billed regardless of outcome. It ensures we don't waste your time or ours.
What changes after 5 PM or on Sundays?
The whole bill (visit + labor) is +40%. A standard minimum job becomes about LKR 8,400 after hours. We confirm this on the call before we move. No surprises.
What if it can't be fixed in one visit?
You pay for the time and parts used so far at published rates. Follow-up visits are scheduled at standard rates — no penalty pricing. Honest work, honest billing.
