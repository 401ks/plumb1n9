COMMON CAUSES

Scale-Choked Lines

Mineral buildup narrows pipes over time. A booster pump on a choked line just moves the restriction louder.

Dying Pumps & Valves

Pump motors weaken and stuck valves restrict flow. Both mimic mains-side loss — we test to tell you which.

Mains-Side Loss

If the loss is the board's side of the meter, we tell you honestly and point you there — we don't bill you for their problem.

PRO TIPS

Pressure Care Tips

Before We Arrive

  • • Test taps at multiple points — upstairs, downstairs, kitchen, bathroom.
  • • Check if neighbours have the same issue — that points to mains-side.
  • • Note when it started — sudden vs gradual helps us diagnose faster.

Common Mistakes

  • • Don't install a booster pump without diagnosing the cause first.
  • • Don't assume it's the board — test at your meter first.
  • • Don't ignore sudden drops — they usually mean a leak or valve failure.
