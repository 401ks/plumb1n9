Plumbing Dispatch & Pricing FAQs

Straight answers about our dispatch network — the LKR 5,000 deployment fee, contractor hourly rates, 60-minute emergency response, and the 6-month workmanship shield.

Dispatch & Pricing FAQs

How does your dispatch process work?

WhatsApp us a 10-second video of the issue. Our dispatch center verifies the emergency, quotes the LKR 5,000 deployment fee plus the applicable hourly rate, and deploys the nearest vetted contractor. You pay only after you approve the work.

How much is the LKR 5,000 deployment fee?

The flat LKR 5,000 fee covers immediate vehicle dispatch, fuel, and the contractor's first-hour diagnostic. It guarantees your 60-minute arrival slot and is non-refundable, ensuring we only deploy to serious, verified emergencies.

What are the standard contractor hourly rates?

Standard plumbing (taps, leaks, visible pipes): LKR 3,000/hr. Wastewater blockages (jetting, camera): LKR 5,000/hr. Sewer / raw sewage / biohazard: LKR 8,000/hr with hazard premium. After-hours and weekend dispatches carry a +40% surcharge.

What is the 6-Month Workmanship Shield?

Every contractor repair is backed by our shield. If a repair fails within 6 months, we dispatch a fix free. Exclusions: pre-existing pipe degradation, acts of nature, or third-party damage.

What qualifies as a plumbing emergency?

Active flooding, burst pipes, sewage backup, complete water loss, or gas leaks. Send a WhatsApp video for instant triage, and if verified we deploy a specialist within 60 minutes.

How quickly can you respond to emergencies in Colombo?

For verified emergencies we dispatch within 60 minutes across Colombo and the Western Province network. Send a 10-second WhatsApp video so we can verify, quote, and deploy immediately.

Do you charge extra for traveling to my area?

No. The LKR 5,000 deployment fee covers dispatch and travel across our entire Western Province network. After-hours and weekend dispatches carry a +40% surcharge.

Can I get a fixed quote before you arrive?

We provide rate ranges based on your description and confirm the final scope on-site after diagnosis. Rates are confirmed via WhatsApp before deployment. Our zero-tolerance policy means price disputes after completed work are not entertained.

Are your contractors licensed and vetted?

Yes. Every contractor in our network is licensed, insured, and background-checked before deployment. We dispatch contractors by name, never anonymous handymen.
